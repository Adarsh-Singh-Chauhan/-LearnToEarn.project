THE IDEA / 01

Consistency should compound.

LearnToEarn makes the daily habit visible, measurable and verifiable—not hidden behind a central database.

01 / CHECK IN
◷

One claim per day

Connect an EVM wallet and submit a daily check-in. V2 uses UTC calendar days to prevent duplicate claims.

02 / BUILD
⌁

Build your streak

Claim on consecutive days to grow the streak. Miss a calendar day and the next successful claim starts again at day one.

03 / EARN
✳

Earn from a shared pool

Rewards are paid in the selected chain's native currency, when the pool is funded and the configured schedule grants a bonus.

WHY IT EXISTS / 02

Good habits fade
when progress is invisible.

Daily learning and skill-building need consistency. Yet many habit systems store progress in a private database, offer little transparency around reward funding and make it hard for participants to verify how incentives are calculated.

THE LEARNTOEARN APPROACH

Record streak state and reward rules in a smart contract, expose pool balances on-chain, and let users inspect the rules before interacting.

SYSTEM DESIGN / 04

Simple flow.
Verifiable rules.

The website is a client interface. The Solidity contract is the source of truth for streak state, reward schedule and pool balance.

→

03LearnToEarn V2Validates streak + reward

↳ Every state change and reward event is recorded by the selected EVM network. The frontend reads the contract; it does not calculate or store authoritative streaks.
